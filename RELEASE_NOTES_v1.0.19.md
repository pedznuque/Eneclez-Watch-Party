## Eneclez Watch Party v1.0.19

- Watch torrent videos from magnet links or .torrent files, with video search, transfer statistics, and progress for the selected video.
- Improved buffering and fixed playback stopping early, pause/resume, seeking, and disabled controls after loading.
- Added an embedded subtitle selector with text captions and PGS image subtitle support, including subtitle timing after seeking.
- Added the playing title, fullscreen Show/Hide controls, and a Keep visible option.
- Improved room sharing confirmation, late joining, controller permissions, and recovery from connection errors.
- Refreshed the torrent interface for desktop and compact layouts.

The room server has already been updated for torrent sharing. Everyone watching torrents needs this updated desktop app and their own connection to reachable peers. Download speed depends on the available peers.

To update, open Settings and choose Check for updates, then restart when prompted. You can also install the attached Windows installer.
